hello, world!

I'm Filip, a software engineer with many, many interests (way too many actually). Primarily: game networking, processes and management.

In my personal life I'm a happy husband and a father of two. I also play games and read a book from time to time (way too seldom if you ask me).

My motto is "It can't be that hard" which has led me to many great discoveries, one of them being that I can achieve anything with enough time and effort, and another being that many things are actually harder than they seem at first.

I like reinventing the wheel. Using the wheel does not necessarily mean you understand how it works. Creating the wheel on the other hand usually forces the creator to understand it in depth. I like understanding how things work.

I'm a strong believer of the iterative approach and Gall's Law. Start simple. Release early. Gather feedback. Iterate, iterate, iterate!
