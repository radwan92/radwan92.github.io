FAQ

  • Why not mention technologies or languages used?

    I don't think that tech is a major obstacle. I've seen whole teams swiftly switch to new languages and new frameworks with big success. I've also seen people claiming proficiency in a multitude of technologies struggle with the most basic tasks. Ability to learn, adapt and understand is what I think matters, and it's not easy to prove that on a resume.
